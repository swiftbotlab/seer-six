For builders

Outcome tokens are ordinary tokens

Every Seer market sits on Gnosis Conditional Tokens. Collateral splits into one token per outcome, and each position is wrapped as an ERC-20, so it trades on ordinary pools, sits in any wallet and can be read by any contract.

  • Split 1 sDAI into one token of every outcome.
  • Merge a full set back into 1 sDAI at any time.
  • Redeem winning tokens for collateral after the answer is final.
  • Create a market without permission, on any of the four chains.

Design notes

Design rationaleAll notes for the six interfaces
  1. One question, many priced answers. The kit's line motif runs the way Seer's Part II brief asks: one line enters from the left and fans out to the right. It is drawn from the most liquid open market on Seer, read live: the line is the question, each band an answer, and a band's width is its price (a full set of outcome tokens is worth 1 collateral, so the widths add up to the whole).
  2. Two sides of every market. Under the hero, the information seeker's path (ask, a market prices it, use the signal) sits beside the trader's (find, understand, trade on Seer), with one market drawn between them: Seer as a two-sided marketplace, in the structure of the page.
  3. A protocol map replaces the marketing grid. The raven art, the category pill cloud and the feature cards become one diagram: five apps above the building blocks they share.
  4. One worked example instead of claims. A fictional grant round is followed through four apps with the same market IDs they use. No invented protocol totals.
  1. Typefaces. Geist and Geist Mono stand in for PP Neue Montreal and Suisse Int'l Mono when those are not installed. Accent words stay in the sans, in violet.
  2. Colour. Muted text is lifted to pass WCAG AA. Status amber and red appear only on market states, next to their label; charts use the system palette, violet first.
  3. Two poster moments. The ink protocol layer and the violet closing poster come from the kit's posters; everything else stays on calm kit surfaces.
