    How it works · Mkt #2842

    One pool, several winners

    Sample data
    01

    Every entry is an outcome token

    The 40 proposals and an Invalid outcome are the outcomes of one multi-categorical market. Each position is an ERC-1155 token wrapped as an ERC-20, so it trades in an ordinary pool. One full set always redeems for 1 sDAI, so the prices add up to 100%.

    02

    Funded entries split the payout

    Several outcomes can be true. If the panel funds eight proposals, each funded share redeems for 1/8 sDAI and every other share for nothing.

    03

    Reality.eth records the decision

    When the panel publishes its scores, anyone can answer the market's Reality.eth question with the funded list, backed by a bond. A challenged answer goes to Kleros. If the round is cancelled, the market resolves Invalid.

    Design notes · 05 Opportunity

    What we kept, what we changed

    Kept from the Electric Foundation kit

    • Tokens, radii and type roles as specified: card 9, bet slip 22, controls 8; Medium 13 UI, Medium 12 meta, Bold 32 amounts.
    • The market card's anatomy: glyph tile, two-line title, outcome pricing, and the "$Vol • time • category" footer with a bookmark.
    • The bet slip as drawn: two price tiles, an amount with +$ chips, Confirm with a clear button.
    • The "data barcode" bars and the ink poster, with mono spec labels around them.

    Departures on this page, and why

    1. The barcode carries data. Each bar in the hero is one entry; its length is the chance of selection, on a square-root scale so the long tail stays visible. Violet bars sit inside the eight funding slots, and the dashed rule is the selection line.
    2. Two numbers per entry. In a market with several winners, price and probability differ, so each card shows the chance of selection first and the share price second.
    3. Buy and Sell replace Yes and No. Each entry is one outcome token, as in Seer's live Opportunity ticket. The +1 / +5 / +10 / +100 chips and 0.5% slippage come from that ticket too.
    4. Payout math for split winners. The ticket prices a funded share at 1/8 sDAI, not 1 sDAI, and warns when a thin pool would push an order past its slippage.
    5. A reviewer view. The kit has no sponsor screens. The review queue uses only violet tints and labels; there are no green or red verdict colours, because the market does not decide.
    6. Kit fixes. Decimal points, not commas; muted text lifted to pass AA contrast.
