        Design notes · Deep

        What we kept, what we changed

        Kept from the Electric Foundation kit

        • Tokens, radii and type roles exactly: card 9, bet slip 22, controls 8; Medium 13 UI, Medium 12 meta, Bold 16/24 titles, Bold 32 amounts.
        • The kit's binary pair for scalar outcomes: violet Up as primary, tinted Down as secondary, in rows and in the ticket.
        • The bet slip as drawn, with two price tiles, +$ chips and Confirm with a clear button.
        • The stacked bell-curve waves and the ink poster panel, with mono spec labels around them.

        Departures on this page, and why

        1. The waves carry data. The chart at the top draws all 40 proposals as stacked waves. Each peak sits at a proposal's forecast score, and its height shows how much forecasters agree. The eight funded ranks are violet; the rest fade by rank, as in the poster.
        2. Up and Down replace Yes and No. A score is a number, not an event, so each outcome trades as a pair: UP pays the score ÷ 10.
        3. Distribution sparklines in table rows. The kit has no tables. We kept them quiet: one hue, a dashed funding line, no gridlines.
        4. An Agents view set in mono. The same rows as a manifest and CSV, so agents and people read one market with no private feed.
        5. Semantic colour only where it means something. Green and red appear only on signed numbers (24-hour moves, edge, P&L). Amber marks contested proposals in the funding signal, with a legend. Muted text is lifted to pass AA contrast.
