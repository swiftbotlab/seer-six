Method

How to read the board.

01

What counts as a default

Seer's Risk Pricing definition, which the market resolves against:

  • Redemption failure. The asset can't be redeemed at its expected value for 2 weeks.
  • Reserve insolvency. Reserves sit 1% or more below issuance value for 7 days or longer.
  • Other structural failures relevant to how the token works: wrappers, liquid staking tokens, fiat-backed stablecoins.

Only defaults that happen during the quarter count. Defaults caused by events before it don't.

02

From token price to yearly PD

Each asset has a token in market #2850, and so does No to All. At resolution the sDAI is split between the assets that defaulted; if none did, No to All takes it. So a token's price is close to the market's chance of a default this quarter, a little under it because the payout is shared when several assets default (here × 0.89, the factor that makes all 34 prices add up to 1 sDAI). The board shows the chance per year.

03

Four bands, as reading aids

Bands group yearly estimates so a long board scans quickly. They are not ratings. The number and its range are the signal.

    04

    How it resolves

    When the quarter ends, the question opens on Reality.eth. Anyone can answer with a bond; a challenged answer goes to Kleros. Once the answer is final, tokens redeem for sDAI.

    Design notes · Risk Pricing

    Kept from the Electric Foundation kit, and where this page departs

    Kept

    • Kit tokens in both themes: page, card and nested surfaces, violet primary, the secondary No/Down button.
    • Radii 9 / 22 / 8, Medium 13 UI type, Medium 12 meta, Bold 16/24 titles and the Bold 32 amount in the slip.
    • Kit components: pill search, category row with arrows, segmented controls, bet slip with +$ chips and Confirm + ✕.
    • Mono spec labels for market metadata, one serif-italic accent phrase per headline.

    Departures

    1. Lines carry data. The hero's EF line runs the way Seer's Part II brief draws it: one stem enters from the left (the question) and fans out into one lane per asset group, each as thick as its share of the 33 assets; the estimates sit on those lanes. The 45° line field marks the bands, its density rising with each band; in No to All each stripe is one asset, as wide as its share of the board's risk.
    2. Name. Risk Pricing stays the product name, so it reads as Seer's app. The small "proposed name: Barometer" label is our name proposal for Seer (poidh #39): a barometer is the instrument you read to see pressure building before the weather turns.
    3. A log scale, not bars from zero. Estimates run from 0.07% to 14%. On a linear axis most of the board sits in one pixel; on a log scale twice as risky is always the same step.
    4. Ranges next to every price. Each estimate carries the 10th–90th percentile of the estimates traders set when they trade (size-weighted), so a 10% asset with a wide range reads differently from a tight one.
    5. Band colours, used once. The kit has no status colours. Green to red marks the four bands only, always beside a label and a distinct shape. Prices stay violet.
    6. Real board first, fictional walkthrough. The hero is Seer's own board, read from risk.seer.pm. The walkthrough uses invented assets, so no real protocol is paired with an invented estimate. Seer's default definition, bands and pricing mechanics are kept as published.
