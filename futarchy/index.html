    What we kept, and where we departed

    Kept from the Electric Foundation kit

    • The kit's dark and light UI tokens, violet #7D33FF for primary actions, and the secondary violet tint for the second choice.
    • Bet-slip geometry: 22px panel, 16px padding, 18px gap, two price tiles, a Bold 32 amount, + chips, Confirm and a clear button.
    • Medium 13 for UI, Medium 12 for meta, Bold 16/24 for titles, and mono spec labels for market metadata.
    • The official EF lockup in the header, and the logo sheet's 45° construction lines and circles behind the hero fork.

    Departures on this page

    1. The fork is a chart. Its two branches end at the real 5-day TWAPs on a true price scale, and the ±1% band around the NO price is drawn in, so the signal reads before any number does.
    2. The serif accent marks the condition (“if … is approved?”), the same clause the live product highlights.
    3. Prices are in conditional sDAI, not cents. Each pool trades YES_LAT against YES_sDAI (and NO_LAT against NO_sDAI), not a probability, so the kit's ¢ tiles carry four-decimal prices and a colour key per branch.
    4. Green and red appear only on the signed impact and the signal label. YES is violet and NO is kit lavender on every chart, as on the other five interfaces.
    5. The ticket shows both futures. A conditional trade is undone in the branch that does not happen, so the slip states what you hold if approved and if rejected.
    6. The window scrubber is exploratory. The official signal always uses the fixed 5-day window; other windows are labelled as yours.

    Sample data: Lattice, Harbor and Kestrel DAOs are fictional. LIP-31 is the same proposal the other five interfaces reference.
