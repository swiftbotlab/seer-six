    Kept

    • The mark. The bolt and the wordmark come from the designer's own vectors: recoloured, never redrawn.
    • Tokens and geometry. Page #101013, card #1A1A1E, primary #7D33FF, active chip #8F55F7; radius 9 for cards, 22 for panels, 8 for controls.

    Changed, and why

    • One sans in headlines. The kit's web screens use a single sans; its Reckless italic lives in the social frames. Here the accent phrase turns violet in the same face.
    • An 11.5px type floor. No HTML text is smaller and chart labels are at least 11px, so dense boards stay readable on a phone.
    • Muted text, lifted. #67676F becomes #8A8A94 on dark and #8E8E99 becomes #6E6E79 on light, so 12px labels pass WCAG AA.
    • Motifs that carry data. Wedge widths are outcome shares, waves are forecast distributions, the 45° fork is a futarchy decision.
    • Each price in its market's unit. A scalar reads 8.14/10 and a multi-winner market 50% · 6.3¢ of 12.5¢. Binary prices add up to 100¢; the kit's slip shows 69¢ + 37¢.

    All design notesthe long version · kept from the kit · deliberate departures

    Kept from the Electric Foundation kit

    1. The official mark and wordmark, taken from the designer's own vector files and recoloured, never redrawn.
    2. The UI tokens as specified: page #101013 / #F7F7F9, card #1A1A1E / #FFFFFF, nested #1F1F24 / #F1F1F4, primary #7D33FF, secondary #241E33 / #EFE9FC, active chip #8F55F7.
    3. The geometry: radius 9 for cards, 22 for panels, 8 for buttons and chips; 12px card padding; 40px page gutter and 32px grid gap on desktop.
    4. The type spec: Medium 13 for UI, Medium 12 for meta, Bold 16/24 for market titles, Bold 32 at −2% for amounts.
    5. The kit's components: pill search, category row with arrows, Yes/No outcome rows, Up/Down and three-way buttons, and the bet slip with +$ chips.
    6. The graphic language: converging wedges, stacked waves, the dot grid that becomes a line grid, 45° construction lines, data bars and mono spec labels.

    Deliberate departures

    1. Motifs carry data. Wedge widths are outcome shares, waves are forecast distributions, the 45° fork is a futarchy decision, data bars are a field of entries.
    2. Typefaces. PP Neue Montreal and Suisse Int'l Mono are used when installed; everyone else gets licensed stand-ins, Geist and Geist Mono (it keeps the slashed zero). Headlines use one sans: the accent words are violet, not a second, serif face.
    3. Contrast. Small muted text is lifted from #67676F to #8A8A94 in dark mode and from #8E8E99 to #6E6E79 in light mode, so 12px labels pass WCAG AA.
    4. Semantic colour, used sparingly. The kit has no green, amber or red. We add them only for price moves and risk bands, always next to a sign or a label. Futarchy's NO branch is the kit's lavender, so "rejected" never reads as a positive colour.
    5. A chart palette. Six colours, violet first, checked for colour-vision deficiency in both themes. Anything past six becomes "Other".
    6. Small kit fixes. The kit's bet slip shows Yes 69¢ and No 37¢, which add up to 106; our binary prices add up to 100. "$100,00" is written "$100.00".
    7. Each price in its market's unit. The kit reads every price as a percent, but most Seer types don't work that way. A scalar round shows an expected score (8.14/10 · UP 81¢), a multi-winner market leads with the chance and reads the price against the payout (50% · 6.3¢ of 12.5¢: a funded share pays 1/8 sDAI), and the risk board shows a default chance per year. Tickets pay out the same way.
    8. An ecosystem strip above every header. One protocol, one wallet, six apps: the strip is the same on every page and marks where you are.
