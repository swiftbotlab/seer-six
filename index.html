The kit, applied

Component pairs

Kept

  • The logo stays Seer's. The raven from Seer's X profile, path data from app.seer.pm's own SVG, beside the lowercase seer of its X banner; a derived mark is offered as an option (keep or derive).
  • Tokens. #101013 · #1A1A1E · #7D33FF; radius 9 · 22 · 8.
  • Components. Pill search, chips, outcome rows, bet slip.

Changed, and why

  • One sans. The accent phrase is violet, not a serif.
  • 11.5px floor, so dense boards read on a phone.
  • Muted text lifted to #8A8A94 to pass WCAG AA.
  • Lines fan out, one → many, left to right, as the Part II brief asks.
  • Motifs carry data: shares, forecasts, a decision fork.
  • Prices in their unit: 8.14/10, 50% · 6.3¢ of 12.5¢.

Where the data comes from

  • Live: the top of Home (the most liquid open market), the App's “On Seer now” strip, Futarchy's EIP-8363, Opportunity's AI Job Board Name market and Risk's Q3 2026 board read Seer's public APIs as the page loads. If Seer can't be reached, each shows its snapshot and says so.
  • Refreshed every hour, server side: every snapshot above and Deep's Round 3 field, all 98 repositories (deep.seer.pm, whose feed allows no cross-site reads), read by a scheduled job (tools/refresh.py) with the pages' own checks; a source that fails keeps its last good copy. Fixed records, linked to the source: Futarchy's nine closed proposals (futarchy.seer.pm, read 21 Oct 2026) and the settled Rebrand Contest on Opportunity (app.seer.pm, 20 Oct).
  • For agents: llms.txt and data/*.json, the same Seer data with source, fetch time and mode (more).
  • Sample: the walkthrough under each real hero is one made-up thread (Lattice DAO, P-07, #2841) followed across all six apps, labelled “Sample”; every sample address starts with 0x5eed.

Accessibility and QA

  • Contrast: every text colour is at least 4.6:1 on the page, card and row surfaces, in dark and light (WCAG AA), computed from the tokens.
  • Keyboard: a skip link is the first Tab stop, every control shows a focus ring, and data tables are real tables with headers.
  • Reduced motion: transitions and animations drop to near zero when the system asks.
  • Tested: all eight pages at 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px, dark and light at 390 and 1440: no console errors and no sideways scroll.

    All design notesthe long version · kept from the kit · deliberate departures

    The reasoning behind these, point by point (two sides, the explorer, the three questions, the identity and the logo): Design rationale.

    Kept from the Electric Foundation kit

    1. The UI tokens as specified: page #101013 / #F7F7F9, card #1A1A1E / #FFFFFF, nested #1F1F24 / #F1F1F4, primary #7D33FF, secondary #241E33 / #EFE9FC, active chip #8F55F7.
    2. The geometry: radius 9 for cards, 22 for panels, 8 for buttons and chips; 12px card padding; 40px page gutter and 32px grid gap on desktop.
    3. The type spec: Medium 13 for UI, Medium 12 for meta, Bold 16/24 for market titles, Bold 32 at −2% for amounts.
    4. The kit's components: pill search, category row with arrows, Yes/No outcome rows, Up/Down and three-way buttons, and the bet slip with +$ chips.
    5. The graphic language: the violet line field, stacked waves, the dot grid that becomes a line grid, 45° construction lines, data bars and mono spec labels.

    Deliberate departures

    1. The logo is Seer's current one: the raven in its open ring with the star, as on Seer's X profile (the vector from app.seer.pm's own header SVG, recoloured only), beside the lowercase seer that Seer's X banner sets with its line. The kit carries everything around it; a derived mark that links the two is on the rationale page as an option.
    2. Line direction, reversed. The kit's lines converge from many into one point. Seer's Part II brief asks for the reverse, so every line motif here runs one → many, left to right: one question in, its priced answers out (on this page, one protocol out to six interfaces).
    3. Motifs carry data. Branch widths are answer prices, waves are forecast distributions, the 45° fork is a futarchy decision, data bars are a field of entries.
    4. Typefaces. PP Neue Montreal and Suisse Int'l Mono are used when installed; everyone else gets licensed stand-ins, Geist and Geist Mono (it keeps the slashed zero). Headlines use one sans: the accent words are violet, not a second, serif face.
    5. Contrast. Small muted text is lifted from #67676F to #8A8A94 in dark mode and from #8E8E99 to #686873 in light mode, so 12px labels pass WCAG AA.
    6. Semantic colour, used sparingly. The kit has no green, amber or red. We add them only for price moves and risk bands, always next to a sign or a label. Futarchy's NO branch is the kit's lavender, so "rejected" never reads as a positive colour.
    7. A chart palette. Six colours, violet first, checked for colour-vision deficiency in both themes. Anything past six becomes "Other".
    8. Small kit fixes. The kit's bet slip shows Yes 69¢ and No 37¢, which add up to 106; our binary prices add up to 100. "$100,00" is written "$100.00".
    9. Each price in its market's unit. The kit reads every price as a percent, but most Seer types don't work that way. A scalar round shows an expected score (8.14/10 · UP 81¢), a multi-winner market leads with the chance and reads the price against the payout (50% · 6.3¢ of 12.5¢: a funded share pays 1/8 sDAI), and the risk board shows a default chance per year. Tickets pay out the same way.
    10. One header, one Apps menu. One protocol, one wallet, six apps: every header carries the same Apps button, which lists all six and marks where you are, and one chip that says where the first screen's numbers come from: Live, Snapshot or Sample.
